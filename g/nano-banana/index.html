Nano Banana in Gemini: keep the same face across AI images

Make and edit images in the Gemini app while people's faces stay the same across pictures.

Who it's for

Anyone who makes posters and cards for the family group or a small business.

How to try it

  1. Open the Gemini app or gemini.google.com and sign in.
  2. From the tools menu, choose Create images.
  3. Type a prompt, or upload a photo to edit.
  4. Upload a photo of a person to keep their look consistent across new images.
  5. Paid users can tap the three-dot menu and choose Redo with Pro.

Links

Good to know

  • Newest model is Nano Banana 2.1, for Google AI plan subscribers; free users get Nano Banana 2.
  • Both include character consistency and text rendering; free tier also offers photo combining, precise editing and 2K resolution.
  • Every image gets an invisible SynthID watermark plus a visible AI watermark.
