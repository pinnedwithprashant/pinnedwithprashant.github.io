Block 18+ dating and AI companion apps on your child's iPhone

Apple now rates dating apps and romantic AI companion apps 18+. Set your child's app age limit so these apps are blocked.

Who it's for

Parents who manage a child's iPhone through Family Sharing.

How to try it

  1. On your iPhone, open Settings, then Family, then your child's name.
  2. Tap Content & Privacy Restrictions and enter your Screen Time passcode if asked.
  3. Open the App Store, Media, Web & Games settings and tap Apps.
  4. Choose 16+ or lower. Apps rated above the limit are blocked and hidden.
  5. Check an app's age rating on its App Store page if you are unsure.

Good to know

  • The new declarations start 16 Nov 2026; apps have until 12 Jan 2027, so some keep old ratings for a while.
  • Apps must also declare real-world location sharing with another person or group.
  • Brazil has its own A16 and A18 local ratings.
  • A rating only blocks apps if you set a limit in Screen Time.
