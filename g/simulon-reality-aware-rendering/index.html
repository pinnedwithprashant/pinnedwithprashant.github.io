Try Simulon's Reality-Aware Rendering on iPhone

Simulon lets you place a digital character in your room with your iPhone camera, act out a scene, and get a rendered take where the character reacts.

Who it's for

iPhone users (iOS 17 or later) who make reels, short films or ads.

How to try it

  1. Install Simulon from the App Store (free download).
  2. Sign up; new accounts get 100 one-time starter credits for Library characters.
  3. Pick a character and place it in your room through the camera.
  4. Hit record and act as if it is there: talk to it, move something near it.
  5. Stop recording and let Simulon render the take.
  6. For your own characters from photos, sketches or text, upgrade to Plus or Pro.

Good to know

  • Plus is $14.99/month and Pro $39.99/month on simulon.com; App Store prices can differ.
  • Starter credits are a one-time gift, not a monthly refill.
  • Android is listed as coming next; no date given.
  • Render time and credits per render are not published.
