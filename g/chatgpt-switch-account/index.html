Keep two ChatGPT accounts on one phone

ChatGPT on iPhone and Android can keep two accounts signed in and switch between them.

Who it's for

Anyone with a work and a personal ChatGPT account, or a shared family phone.

How to try it

  1. Open the ChatGPT app and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Switch account.
  3. Tap Add account and sign in to the second account.
  4. To switch later, tap Switch account and pick an account. The active one has a checkmark.

Good to know

  • Maximum 2 accounts; log one out to add another.
  • Each account keeps its own chats, memory, files, settings and plan.
  • A paid plan on one account does not cover the other.
  • Rollout is gradual; you may not see Switch account yet.
  • Work accounts still follow their organisation's rules.
