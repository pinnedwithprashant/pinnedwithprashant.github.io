Fish Audio Drama 3: AI voices that whisper, laugh and pause

Direct an AI voice with tags like [whispering] and [laughing], with up to 16 speakers in one script.

Who it's for

Creators who make story reels, audio dramas or character voiceovers.

How to try it

  1. Go to fish.audio and sign up free.
  2. Open Text to Speech and select Drama 3.
  3. Type your script and choose a voice.
  4. Add tags like [whispering], [laughing] or [pause] where you want them.
  5. Click Generate and play to hear it.

Prompts to copy

Sample script with tags
[whispering] I have a secret. [pause] [laughing] Okay, I will tell you.

Good to know

  • Fish Audio's free plan is for personal use only; paid plans give commercial rights.
  • Supports up to 16 speakers in one text, using speaker tags 0 to 15 (per the Hugging Face model card).
  • Supports 83 languages.
  • Cloning a real person's voice without consent is prohibited.
