Explore any scene in Gemini, from planet to atom

Open Gemini's immersive explorations library and tap through layered visual scenes, from the big picture down to molecules and atoms.

Who it's for

Anyone with a personal Google account and the Gemini app on web or mobile. Workspace accounts also have it.

How to try it

  1. Open the Gemini app or go to gemini.google.com and sign in.
  2. Open the menu and tap Students, then tap Try immersive explorations. Or go straight to gemini.google.com/immersive-explorations.
  3. Pick a scene, for example the Silk Road, a jet engine or lightning.
  4. Tap a part of the picture to open more detail.
  5. Keep tapping to go deeper, down to molecules and atoms.
  6. To use Hindi, set your Gemini language to Hindi.

Good to know

  • The library has a fixed set of scenes for now. You cannot ask for any topic yet.
  • Google says chat access and audio narration are coming soon.
  • Languages: English, Hindi, Indonesian, Brazilian Portuguese, French and simplified Chinese. Other languages see English.
  • Works on web and mobile.
