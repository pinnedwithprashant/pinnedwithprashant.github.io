Try PhotoCraft, a free Photoshop-style editor

PhotoCraft is a free, open-source photo editor with Photoshop-style menus, part of ArtCraft's 16 free apps.

Who it's for

Students and small shops who need simple photo edits without a subscription.

How to try it

  1. Open getartcraft.com and download the ArtCraft Launcher for Mac, Windows or Linux.
  2. Install it and open PhotoCraft from the launcher.
  3. Duplicate your photo first, and open the copy, not the original.
  4. Use resize, masks and colour adjustments as you would in Photoshop.
  5. Export the result as a new file.

Good to know

  • Users report critical bugs, including deleted images and merged layers (Engadget). Always work on a copy.
  • It cannot open Photoshop PSD files.
  • It has no AI features.
  • Adobe's terms ban reverse engineering its software; this is ArtCraft's own clean-room code (Engadget).
