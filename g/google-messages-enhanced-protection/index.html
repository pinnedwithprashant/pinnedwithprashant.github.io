Turn on Google Messages Enhanced protection against scam texts

Google Messages has an opt-in Enhanced protection mode. Gemini checks texts from unknown senders for scams.

Who it's for

Android users who use Google Messages, and anyone setting up a parent's phone.

How to try it

  1. Open Google Messages on your Android phone.
  2. Tap your profile picture or initials at the top right.
  3. Tap Messages settings, then Protection & Safety.
  4. If Enhanced protection is listed, select it. Google is still rolling it out.

Links

Good to know

  • Enhanced protection is opt-in. Google says it is rolling out over the coming weeks from 14 October 2026.
  • Scam Detection with Private AI Compute: Pixel 6 and later, Galaxy S24 series and later, Galaxy Z Flip and Z Fold6 and later, and other AICore devices.
  • On-device link checks with Gemini Nano: Pixel 10 and later, Galaxy S26 series, Galaxy Z Flip and Z Fold8, and other select devices.
  • Warnings for unfamiliar links from numbers not in your contacts work on Android 6 and later.
  • If you report spam, Google receives that message for analysis.
