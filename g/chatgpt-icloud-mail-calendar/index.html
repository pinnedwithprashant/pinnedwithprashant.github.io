Connect iCloud Mail and Calendar to ChatGPT (and disconnect it)

ChatGPT now has iCloud Mail and iCloud Calendar plugins on all plans, including free. You connect them with an Apple app-specific password, not your main password.

Who it's for

ChatGPT users (free or paid) with an iCloud address ending in @icloud.com, @me.com or @mac.com, on web, desktop or iOS.

How to try it

  1. Turn on two-factor authentication for your Apple Account.
  2. Go to account.apple.com > Sign-In and Security > App-Specific Passwords and create one for ChatGPT.
  3. In ChatGPT, open Settings > Plugins and find iCloud Mail.
  4. Connect it with your iCloud address and the app-specific password.
  5. Repeat for iCloud Calendar, using your primary Apple Account email.
  6. To stop access, revoke that app-specific password at account.apple.com.

Good to know

  • Mail and Calendar are separate plugins; connect each one.
  • Gmail or Outlook accounts added to Apple's Mail app are not included.
  • ChatGPT can send email; it may ask for approval depending on your settings.
  • Never type your main Apple password into ChatGPT.
