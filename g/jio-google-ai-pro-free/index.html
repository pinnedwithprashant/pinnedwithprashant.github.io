Claim Jio's free Google AI Pro (18 months)

Jio users on an unlimited 5G plan of ₹349 or more can claim 18 months of Google AI Pro free in the MyJio app.

Who it's for

Jio users aged 18+ on an active unlimited 5G plan of ₹349 or above (prepaid or postpaid).

How to try it

  1. Check that your Jio plan is an unlimited 5G plan of ₹349 or above.
  2. Open the MyJio app and log in with your Jio number.
  3. Tap the Google Gemini banner on the home screen.
  4. Register with your Gmail ID to activate Google AI Pro.
  5. Keep your 5G plan active for the whole 18 months to keep the benefit.

Good to know

  • One claim per unique mobile number.
  • The benefit stays only while your unlimited 5G plan of ₹349+ stays active.
  • Includes 5 TB of storage shared across Gmail, Drive and Photos, plus Gemini's Pro features.
  • Jio's page calls it a limited-time offer but shows no end date.
  • Deal sites quoting Jio's terms (DesiDime and others) say claims close 29 Oct 2026, or another date Jio decides.
