Doory: a free design tool for posters, posts and thumbnails

Free, open-source design tool that runs in your browser. Make posters, posts, slides and thumbnails without a paid plan.

Who it's for

Anyone who makes festival posters, Instagram posts, invitations or YouTube thumbnails and does not want to pay for a design app.

How to try it

  1. Open doory.art in any browser on your laptop or phone.
  2. Start designing right away; no account or sign-up is needed.
  3. Pick a format such as a social post, story, slide, poster or thumbnail.
  4. Use the Photos tool to remove a background; it runs inside your browser.
  5. Export your design as PNG, JPG, SVG, PDF, MP4, WebM or GIF.

Good to know

  • Free and open source; described on its site as a free, open-source design platform and a Canva alternative.
  • Guest designs are saved in your browser; a free account adds cloud storage, sharing and brand kits.
  • Licence is the Attribution Assurance License: commercial use is allowed but the author credit must stay.
