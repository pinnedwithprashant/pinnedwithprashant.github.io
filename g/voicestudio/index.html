VoiceStudio: free voice cloning and dubbing on your own computer

Free, open-source app that clones a voice, reads text aloud and dubs video, all on your computer.

Who it's for

Creators who want voiceovers, dubbing or audiobooks without paying for an online voice tool.

How to try it

  1. Open voicestudio.sh or the GitHub Releases page.
  2. Download the installer for your system: Windows .exe, Mac .dmg, or Linux AppImage/.deb.
  3. Install it; expect a trust warning because installers are unsigned and not Apple-notarised.
  4. Open the app and record or upload a short sample of a voice you have permission to use.
  5. Type your text and generate speech; audio stays on your computer in the desktop app.

Good to know

  • Free and open source under AGPL-3.0; a separate closed-source Pro build costs $99 per year or $299 once.
  • About 8 GB of RAM is enough to start; a GPU is optional but speeds things up.
  • Runs on Windows 10/11 x64, macOS and Linux; no native Windows on ARM build; Intel Macs get the app UI only and need a remote backend.
  • Installers are unsigned or ad-hoc signed and not Apple-notarised, so Windows and Mac may show trust warnings; Mac auto-updates are unverified, so update manually.
  • Clone a voice only with the owner's permission; impersonation and fraud are prohibited.
