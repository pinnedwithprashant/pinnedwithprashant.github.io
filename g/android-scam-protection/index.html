Turn on Android's new scam protection in Google Messages

Google added Gemini-powered scam checks to Android. Here is how to switch on the protection that is available now.

Who it's for

Anyone who gets "Hi, is this you?" texts from unknown numbers, and the family members who get them too.

How to try it

  1. Update Google Messages from the Play Store.
  2. In Messages, tap your profile picture, then Messages settings, then Protection & Safety.
  3. Turn on Enable spam protection under Spam protection.
  4. Turn on Enhanced protection mode when it reaches your phone; it is opt-in and off by default.
  5. If the dial pad warns you about a call-forwarding code, do not continue.

Links

Google blog: New Android scam protectionsblog.google/security/new-android-scam-protections/Google Messages Help: spam protectionsupport.google.com/messages/answer/9327903

Good to know

  • Announced on Google's blog on 14 October 2026.
  • Enhanced protection mode is a new opt-in setting; it uses cloud Gemini to check chats with unknown senders inside Private AI Compute.
  • Enhanced protection is rolling out "in the coming weeks".
  • Call-forwarding protection blocks apps from silently turning on forwarding and warns on manually typed forwarding codes; arrives early December on Pixel and other select devices.
