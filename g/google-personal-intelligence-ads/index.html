Check if your Gmail and Photos can shape Google ads

Google's privacy policy now lets connected Gmail and Photos data shape ads. Check what you have connected in a few minutes.

Who it's for

Anyone who uses Gmail or Google Photos and has tried Gemini or AI Mode in Google Search.

How to try it

  1. On your computer, open Search Services Personalization in your Google Account.
  2. Go to Additional Personal Intelligence, then Connected Content Apps.
  3. See if Google Workspace (Gmail) or Google Photos is connected, and decide if you want that.
  4. In Gmail, open Settings, See all settings, General, then Manage Workspace smart feature settings.
  5. Check the setting called Smart features in other Google products.
  6. Review or delete your Search Services History at activity.google.com.

Good to know

  • Google's policy, effective 16 October 2026, says it does not show personalized ads from Drive, Gmail or Photos content, unless you make that data available to Google services that support personalized ads.
  • Connecting content apps is optional and needs Personalized Recommendations turned on. It is for people aged 18 and older.
  • Google Calendar connection is US-only in English. Google Photos connection is not available in all regions.
  • Disconnecting an app or deleting its data does not delete your Search Services History. Manage that at activity.google.com.
  • Google says Search Services History may still be used to improve products, including training generative AI models.
