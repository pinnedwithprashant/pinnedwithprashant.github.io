Choose which apps Siri AI can see on iPhone (iOS 27.2)

Use the new Excluded Apps setting to keep Siri, Search and suggestions away from your banking app, chats or any app you pick.

Who it's for

iPhone 15 Pro, 15 Pro Max or iPhone 16 and later users on iOS 27.2 with Siri AI.

How to try it

  1. Open Settings > General > Software Update and install iOS 27.2.
  2. Open Settings > Siri > Excluded Apps.
  3. Tap Add Apps, tick each app to exclude, then tap the checkmark at the top right.
  4. To remove an app from the list, tap Edit, the red circle, Delete, then the checkmark.

Good to know

  • One list controls Siri, Search and suggestions together for each app.
  • An excluded app may lose voice features such as drafting a message by voice.
  • When an excluded app is open, Siri cannot answer questions about what is on its screen.
  • Siri AI is English-only in India at launch and needs an iPhone 15 Pro or newer.
