Build a household budget in Claude Sheets from one sentence

Describe the budget you want and Claude builds a spreadsheet with tabs, formulas and totals you can keep editing.

Who it's for

Anyone who pays for Claude and tracks home, shop or event money

How to try it

  1. Open Claude (web, desktop or mobile) on a paid plan.
  2. Type: 'Create a monthly household budget spreadsheet in rupees with tabs for income, fixed bills, daily spending and savings.'
  3. Check the tabs and formulas Claude lists for each change.
  4. Change one number, for example a bill, and watch the totals update.
  5. Ask: 'Add a chart of spending by category.'
