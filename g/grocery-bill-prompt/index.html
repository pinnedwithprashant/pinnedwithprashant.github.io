Use ChatGPT or Gemini to cut your grocery bill

Tell an AI chatbot what you spend on groceries and ask for ways to spend less without eating less.

Who it's for

Anyone who does the weekly or monthly grocery shopping for a home.

How to try it

  1. Open ChatGPT or Gemini and start a new chat.
  2. Type how much you spend on groceries each month, in rupees.
  3. Ask for tips to spend less without eating less.
  4. Ask follow-ups, such as cheaper protein swaps or what to buy frozen.
  5. Try two or three tips and compare your next bill.

Prompts to copy

Grocery budget prompt
I usually spend [amount] rupees a month on groceries for [number] people. What are the 7 best tips to spend less on groceries without eating less?
Follow-up
Which of these tips would save me the most in an Indian city? Give me a simple plan for next month.

Good to know

  • In the Tom's Guide test, the writer's bill fell from about $400 to $300.56 in one month. Your result will differ.
  • Tips that worked for him: store brands for staples, comparing unit prices, cheaper proteins, frozen produce, and whole produce instead of pre-cut.
  • Do not paste card numbers, your address or your phone number into a chatbot.
  • Check any price or product claim yourself. Chatbots can be wrong.
