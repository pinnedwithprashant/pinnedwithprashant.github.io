SolidCraft: a free 3D CAD app that runs in your browser

Free, open-source 3D CAD for simple parts, in the browser or on desktop, and AI agents can drive it.

Who it's for

Engineering students and makers who need simple 3D parts without a paid CAD licence.

How to try it

  1. Open solidcraft.io/app to use it in your browser with no install.
  2. Or download the desktop app for Windows, Mac or Linux from solidcraft.io/download.
  3. Sketch a shape, add constraints, then build it into a 3D solid.
  4. Follow the Getting started guide on docs.solidcraft.io for your first part.
  5. Optional: connect an AI agent like Claude through MCP using the official guide.

Good to know

  • Open source, dual-licensed MIT or Apache-2.0; no paid tier found.
  • Described as early but usable for simple parts.
  • Desktop downloads are not code-signed yet, so Windows and Mac show a warning on first launch.
  • AI is optional; the CAD app works without it.
